## About Fluso

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What exactly is Fluso?">
    An AI assistant that connects to your apps — Gmail, Calendar, Slack, GitHub, and a hundred more — and helps you manage work through conversation. It reads your inbox, drafts replies, runs your calendar, creates documents, researches topics, writes code, and can recall saved preferences and project context.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is it just another chatbot?">
    No. A chatbot answers questions. Fluso performs actions: sending mail, scheduling, opening PRs, generating files. External writes follow your [approval settings](/features/approvals); use **Ask me first** and Ask rules to review them before they run.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How is it different from ChatGPT or Claude?">
    Fluso focuses on connected-app work, project files, tasks, and persistent workspace context. Compare the workflows and permissions you need; other assistants can also offer tools and memory.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who is it for?">
    Anyone who loses too much time to email, meetings, research, content creation, or context-switching. Founders, executives, developers, PMs, researchers, and teams all use it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Getting started

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How long does setup take?">
    About five minutes. Account, connect Gmail, start using it. The more apps you connect, the more useful Fluso gets.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which apps should I connect first?">
    Gmail and Calendar. They give you the most immediate value. Add Slack if your team lives there. Add GitHub if you write code.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need technical skills?">
    No. The interface is plain English. If you write code, Fluso can help with that, but you don't need a technical background for email, calendar, tasks, or content.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I get the most out of it?">
    Connect two or three apps. Use it daily for a week. Process a meeting transcript. Try creating a document or deck. Make a project for each major area of work. Most of the value is in the rhythm, not in any single feature. After a couple of weeks, [Going deeper](/going-deeper) is the next step.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Using Fluso

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What can I ask it to do?">
    Anything related to your connected tools and workspace. Summarise the inbox, draft replies, manage the calendar, research topics, create documents and decks, write and review code, analyse data, process meeting transcripts. If it involves your digital work, it probably fits.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How does it know what to do?">
    It understands the request in context. It knows your connected apps, projects, preferences, and conversation history. It picks the right tools — searching email, reading files, calling APIs, generating content — to fulfil your request.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can it handle multiple things at once?">
    Yes. *"Summarise my unread emails, check my calendar for tomorrow, and draft a reply to the client"* gets all three in sequence.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if it doesn't understand?">
    It asks clarifying questions: *"Which project?"* or *"What format?"* Answer for a better result.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What platforms does it run on?">
    Fluso is available for macOS and Windows from [fluso.ai](https://fluso.ai/), and on the [iOS App Store](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/fluso/id6780937477). Some setup controls are desktop-only.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does it work in other languages?">
    Primarily English, but it understands and responds in many. Quality varies by language and task.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Privacy and security

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="When we connect our email and Slack to Fluso, what data can Prem AI access? What changes if we don't use the secure enclave?">
    Fluso accesses your email and Slack content within the permissions you grant. With encrypted inference, model processing happens inside a secure enclave on Prem's infrastructure, without sending your prompts or content to an external model provider.

    The Fluso runtime is separate from encrypted inference. Its data is protected by strict internal access policies and procedures under our SOC 2 Type II compliance program; the runtime itself does not run inside an enclave.

    For greater control, Fluso can run in your own VPC or cloud while using Prem's encrypted inference. Your workspace stays in your infrastructure, and inference content is protected inside the enclave. See [deployment options](/deployment-models).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens to our data when we leave Fluso? What do you retain after we delete our account?">
    Deleting your account deletes your dedicated sandbox and all associated data, including your files, conversations, connected-account credentials, and other records held by Fluso. We do not retain your data after account deletion.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can it see all my emails?">
    It reads email within the permissions you grant, on request and during enabled background task extraction. Retrieved content can become part of conversation history, tasks, or indexed context. See [Legal](/resources/legal) for Fluso's privacy policy.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does it send mail without my permission?">
    Gmail send tools follow your effective **Allow**, **Ask**, or **Deny** policy. Use **Ask me first** with an **Ask** rule to review each draft before it sends; **Allow** or **Allow everything** can continue without a per-action prompt.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where is my data stored?">
    In your isolated workspace on encrypted infrastructure. Files, conversations, tasks, and the knowledge graph are private to you.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I delete my data?">
    You can remove individual workspace files and disconnect apps, or request account deletion through support. Deletion is permanent.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does it train on my data?">
    No. Your private data is never used to train AI models.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if I want to stop using a connected app?">
    Disconnect from **Plugins**: open the connection row's three-dot menu and click **Disconnect**. Tokens are deleted immediately. You can also revoke directly from the app (Google Account, Slack workspace, GitHub apps).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

See [Legal](/resources/legal) for Fluso's privacy policy.

## Pricing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is there a free version?">
    Yes. The free plan is called **Basic** in the app. It includes chat and workspace tools with a smaller usage allowance. Limits use six-hour and weekly windows, rather than a fixed monthly message count. See [Pricing](/resources/pricing).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I try paid features before subscribing?">
    New accounts receive 30 days of Sponsored Confidential access without a paid subscription. That grant is separate from any trial offered at checkout. Check **Settings → Plans** for your effective plan and expiry; confidential inference still needs to be enabled in Settings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens at Free limits?">
    If either usage window is exhausted, Fluso stops new runs and shows the reset time. Wait for that window to reset or choose a higher plan. Reaching a usage limit does not delete your work.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Cancel anytime?">
    You can cancel from your account billing controls. Paid access continues until the end of the paid period. A separate sponsorship keeps its own expiry; otherwise the account returns to Basic.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Plan details on [Pricing](/resources/pricing).

## Skills

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What are skills?">
    Specialised capabilities: creating PDFs, generating images, processing meetings, deep research. They activate when you ask for something they can do.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I install them?">
    Built-in skills are available automatically. You can install marketplace skills or create your own from **Plugins → Skills**. Fluso selects from the skills available to your account when a request matches.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I build my own?">
    Yes. Open **Plugins → Skills**, click **Add skill** at the top right, and pick **Build with Fluso**. The skill-creator skill takes it from there in chat: it asks what workflow the skill should handle, what inputs it takes, what shape the output should have, and which tools or apps are involved, then writes the skill and adds it to **My Skills**. See [Skills](/features/skills#building-your-own) for the full flow.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the plan change which skills I can use?">
    The current skill catalog is shared across plans; usage limits still apply. Skills can require connected apps or other tools. Image generation is unavailable while confidential mode is enabled. See [Skills](/features/skills).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Fluso isn't finding my emails">
    Check Google Workspace is connected (**Plugins**). Try reconnecting from the row's menu. If the issue persists, you may have hit a Gmail API rate limit. Wait a few minutes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The response seems off">
    For an ordinary chat, start a new conversation with **New chat** when the topic has changed. Asking it to ignore earlier messages does not delete that history. Saved Agents keep one main conversation; see [Threads and contexts](/developers/threads-and-contexts) for separate testing or delegated work.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A connected app says it isn't connected">
    Refresh the page or reconnect. OAuth tokens occasionally expire. **Plugins** → row menu → **Reconnect**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I contact support?">
    [support@premai.io](mailto:support@premai.io). For support commitments in a custom deployment, check your Enterprise agreement.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
