Choose Free, Plus, or Pro for Fluso desktop. Plus gives you 10 times the weekly usage allowance of Free. Pro gives you 10 times the weekly usage allowance of Plus and includes confidential mode. Enterprise is available for custom deployments, data residency, and identity or audit integrations.

Fluso uses open-source and open-weight models. Connected apps and other tools use their own services. Your data never trains shared models.

## Pro trial

Eligible new subscribers can start a **30-day Pro trial**. A **payment card is required**. After the trial, Pro renews at **$30/month unless you cancel before the trial ends**.

Free is a separate plan that you can use without a payment card. Open **Settings → Plans** to see the trial offer available to your account, your current plan, and your billing dates.

## What's in every plan

Free, Plus, and Pro share the core workspace features. Your plan determines your weekly usage allowance and access to confidential mode.

- **Chat and workspace tools.** Projects, memory, tasks, connected apps, skills, and document creation.
- **Agents (Beta).** Use saved setups for repeatable work. Agent runs use your plan's allowance. See [Agents](/developers).
- **Open-source and open-weight models.** Availability can change. Connected apps and other tools use their own services.
- **European hosting.** GDPR-aligned by default.
- **Your data is yours.** Nothing trains shared models.

## Weekly usage

Free, Plus, and Pro allowances reset every seven days from when you signed up. Open **Settings → Plans** to see the percentage remaining and next reset date. Chat and Agent activity use the same allowance.

Usage is not a fixed number of messages. A prompt can trigger several model calls, tool steps, or a long conversation history. Short visible replies can still use a substantial allowance.

If you reach your allowance, wait for the next reset, choose a higher plan if one is available, or use [extra usage](#extra-usage) on Plus or Pro. Your existing files and conversations remain available. Storage, service availability, and other operational limits still apply.

Check a stopped task before continuing it. Restored allowance does not mean interrupted work or delayed routines have already resumed. There is no self-service purchase of an extra weekly reset.

## Extra usage

Extra usage lets you keep working on Plus or Pro after your weekly allowance runs out. It's a prepaid balance in USD. Fluso uses it only after your allowance runs out, and only while extra usage is on. When your week resets, Fluso uses your allowance first again and keeps the rest of your balance.

### Buy a pack

Buy a $10 or $25 pack from **Settings → Plans**, the **Weekly limit reached** dialog, or the line above the message box once you've used 80% of the week. You can buy packs on Plus or Pro when your last plan payment went through. Packs aren't sold during the Pro trial or in the iOS app.

You pay by card on a Stripe page. Stripe asks for your billing address, and a business can add its name and VAT ID. Stripe emails you an invoice for each pack, and **Invoices** on **Settings → Plans** lists them. In the desktop app, the Stripe page opens in your browser. Fluso updates your balance once the payment goes through.

Buying a pack turns extra usage on.

### How Fluso uses your balance

- Extra usage pays for the same chats, Agent runs, and routines as your allowance. While it pays, the line above the message box shows what's left.
- Turn extra usage off on **Settings → Plans** to keep your balance for later. Routines keep running while you're away, and they use the balance while extra usage is on.
- Routines that waited at the limit run right away when you buy a pack or turn extra usage on.
- A chat or run that the limit stopped doesn't restart by itself. Send your message again.
- Runs already in progress can use a little more than was left, so your balance can end slightly below zero. Your next pack pays this first.
- Gift credit from Fluso is used before credit you bought. It expires on the date shown on **Settings → Plans**.

### Expiry, refunds, and plan changes

- Each pack lasts 12 months from the day you buy it. **Settings → Plans** shows when each pack expires.
- If a pack is refunded or its payment is disputed, Fluso removes that amount from what's left of the pack.
- If you move to Free, Fluso keeps your balance but doesn't use it. The balance works again when you're back on Plus or Pro, until it expires.

    Fluso uses open-source and open-weight models. Check the available modes in the app. Connected apps and other tools use their own services.
