Fluso's current personal plans are Basic, Plus, Pro, and Confidential. Basic is the free plan. Open **Settings → Plans** to see your effective access, current usage, and the offers available to your account.

Fluso uses only open-source models across all features. Your data is not used to train shared models.

## Plans

| Plan | Monthly price | Usage allowance | Confidential mode access |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | Free | For light, occasional use | No |
| Plus | $19 | Higher than Basic | No |
| Pro | $99 | Higher than Plus | No |
| Confidential | $119 | Higher than Pro | Yes, enable it in Settings |
| Enterprise | Custom | Agreed for the deployment | Agreed for the deployment |

Prices above are the current monthly personal-plan prices. Checkout shows the applicable trial, tax, billing date, and total before you subscribe.

Basic runtimes can sleep when idle and resume when you return. Paid plans keep the runtime available for ongoing work, subject to maintenance and service availability.

The current app does not divide its skill catalog into separate Free and Pro catalogs. Skills, files, connected apps, and background work still depend on their own permissions, configuration, and available usage. Image generation and background task extraction are disabled while confidential mode is enabled.

## Usage limits

Usage is measured in **six-hour** and **weekly** windows. If either window is exhausted, Fluso blocks new runs and shows when that window resets. You can wait for the reset or choose a plan with a higher allowance.

These are usage allowances, not a fixed number of monthly messages or a guaranteed number of tokens per task. Long conversations, model output, and multi-step work use different amounts. The app shows the remaining percentage and reset time for each window.

Reaching a usage limit does not delete your files or conversations. Storage has a separate limit; an upload or write can fail even when inference usage remains.

## Sponsored Confidential

An active **Sponsored Confidential** grant provides Confidential access until its displayed expiry. New accounts receive an initial 30-day grant. A sponsorship is an entitlement from the Fluso team, separate from a paid subscription or a checkout trial.

<Note>
  **Pro does not include confidential mode.** A Pro subscriber can have a separate Sponsored Confidential grant. When that grant expires, Pro alone does not keep confidential access. Sponsorship does not cancel or pause an existing paid subscription.
</Note>

Clerk's billing view describes your paid subscription. Fluso's **Settings → Plans** also accounts for sponsored access, so the two labels can differ. Check the sponsorship expiry as well as the billing renewal date.

## Confidential mode

Having access does not turn encrypted inference on automatically. With the **Confidential** plan or active **Sponsored Confidential** access, enable **Settings → Encrypted Inference**.

Confidential mode processes model inference inside a hardware-isolated enclave. The Fluso runtime is separate from encrypted inference. See [Confidential mode](/features/confidential) for activation, limitations, and the trust boundary.

## Enterprise

Enterprise deployments are scoped with the Fluso team. Agree on deployment ownership, residency, retention, identity integrations, support, and any additional access requirements before treating them as available.

Prem has attained SOC 2 Type II. See the [current security posture](https://www.premai.io/security-at-prem) and [deployment models](/deployment-models).

You can create your own workspace skills from **Plugins → Skills**. Enterprise implementation work is a separate engagement with the team; see [Skills](/features/skills).

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where is my data stored?">
    Fluso Cloud stores workspace data in the EU by default. Model inference and connected apps have their own processing boundaries. Enterprise deployments can scope alternative residency. See [Legal](/resources/legal).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="When is Pro useful?">
    Pro gives intensive work more usage headroom than Plus. It does not add confidential mode. Choose Confidential if you need that access after a sponsorship ends.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I switch or cancel?">
    Use your account's billing controls. Review the effective date and charge shown before confirming. Paid access continues through the paid period after cancellation; a separate sponsorship keeps its own expiry.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Pro guarantee constant background task creation?">
    No. Background extraction depends on connected apps, deployment settings, runtime availability, and service health. It is disabled in confidential mode. Check [Tasks](/features/tasks) for sync activity and explicit task requests.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I get a custom setup or discount?">
    [Email the team](mailto:sales@premai.io) with your requirements and organization details.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
