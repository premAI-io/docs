Choose Free, Plus, or Pro for Fluso desktop. Plus gives you 10 times the weekly usage allowance of Free. Pro gives you 10 times the weekly usage allowance of Plus and includes confidential mode. Enterprise is available for custom deployments, data residency, and identity or audit integrations.

Fluso uses open-source and open-weight models. Connected apps and other tools use their own services. Your data never trains shared models.

## Pro trial

Eligible new subscribers can start a **30-day Pro trial**. A **payment card is required**. After the trial, Pro renews at **$30/month unless you cancel before the trial ends**.

Free is a separate plan that you can use without a payment card. Open **Settings → Plans** to see the trial offer available to your account, your current plan, and your billing dates.

<Note>
  **Enterprise.** Prem has attained SOC 2 Type II. [Review the current security posture.](https://www.premai.io/security-at-prem)

## What's in every plan

Free, Plus, and Pro share the core workspace features. Your plan determines your weekly usage allowance and access to confidential mode.

- **Chat and workspace tools.** Projects, memory, tasks, connected apps, skills, and document creation.
- **Agents (Beta).** Use saved setups for repeatable work. Agent runs use your plan's allowance. See [Agents](/developers).
- **Open-source and open-weight models.** Availability can change. Connected apps and other tools use their own services.
- **European hosting.** GDPR-aligned by default.
- **Your data is yours.** Nothing trains shared models.

## Weekly usage

Free, Plus, and Pro allowances reset every seven days from when you signed up. Open **Settings → Plans** to see the percentage remaining and next reset date. Chat and Agent activity use the same allowance.

Usage is not a fixed number of messages. A prompt can trigger several model calls, tool steps, or a long conversation history. Short visible replies can still use a substantial allowance.

If you reach your allowance, wait for the next reset or choose a higher plan if one is available. Your existing files and conversations remain available. Storage, service availability, and other operational limits still apply.

Check a stopped task before continuing it. Restored allowance does not mean interrupted work or delayed routines have already resumed. There is no self-service purchase of an extra weekly reset.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What models does Fluso use?">
    Fluso uses open-source and open-weight models. Check the available modes in the app. Connected apps and other tools use their own services.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where is my data stored?">
    Fluso Cloud stores workspace data in the EU by default. Model inference and connected-App processing have their own provider boundaries. Enterprise customers can scope alternative data residency. Your data is never used to train shared models. See [Legal](/resources/legal) for Fluso's privacy policy.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Plus vs Pro: when is Pro worth it?">
    Plus costs $10/month and gives you 10 times the weekly usage allowance of Free. Pro costs $30/month, gives you 10 times the weekly usage allowance of Plus, and includes confidential mode. Choose Pro if you need more usage than Plus or want confidential inference.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I switch plans anytime?">
    Yes. Open **Settings → Plans** to upgrade, downgrade, or cancel. Upgrades take effect immediately. Paid-plan downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing period. Review the amount and effective date shown before confirming a change.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is confidential mode?">
    Pro includes model inference inside a hardware-isolated enclave. The Fluso runtime and connected apps have separate processing boundaries. See [Confidential mode](/features/confidential) for how it works and what it protects.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Enterprise security?">
    Identity, retention, audit records, and custom deployment requirements must be agreed with the team. They are not self-service features of a standard Cloud plan. Prem has attained SOC 2 Type II. [Review the current security posture.](https://www.premai.io/security-at-prem)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Custom skills?">
    You can create workspace skills on Free, Plus, and Pro. See [Skills](/features/skills). Custom skills built with the Fluso team can be scoped as part of an Enterprise engagement.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Startup or nonprofit discounts?">

</AccordionGroup>
