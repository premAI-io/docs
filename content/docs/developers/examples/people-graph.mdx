---
title: Find people you know
icon: users
description: Build a contact map you can search, correct, and keep up to date.
---

You remember talking to someone at a company, but cannot remember who. A contact map can help you find that connection.

An Agent can build one from your email and give you a page to search. This is a file it creates for you, separate from Fluso's own memory.

## Build a useful first version

Connect [Google Workspace](/integrations/gmail). Give the Agent **Read only** access if you only want it to read mail.

Describe the job:

> *"Help me find people I've spoken with. Build a searchable contact map from my email, grouped by company. Show where each detail came from. Leave anything you don't know blank."*

Start with recent mail:

> *"Use the last month first. Give me a page where I can search by name or company. Tell me what you've covered so far."*

Open the page it returns. Search for someone you know and check their details against the source email.

## Ask a question that matters to you

> *"Who do I know at this company? Show our recent conversations so I can decide who to contact."*

Or:

> *"Which of these people are based in Berlin? Keep confirmed locations separate from guesses."*

A person's employer does not prove where they live. Similar names do not prove two records are the same person.

Correct the map in the same conversation. Ask **Manager** to save rules that should guide future research.

## Add to it over time

A large mailbox can take more than one run:

> *"Add the previous month's mail next. Keep the contacts you've already found. Save your progress and tell me what's still left."*

Ask for the underlying data as well as the page, so the Agent can improve the same map later.

When the initial review is useful:

> *"Every morning at 8 AM, add anything new since your last completed check. Keep the earlier contacts and give me a short note about what changed."*

Check that the routine exists and uses the right timezone. A refresh mentioned in the instructions is not yet a scheduled task.

<Accordion title="Add information from public profiles">
  With a web-search connection, you can ask for a focused check:

  > *"Look for public profiles for these five contacts. Match the name and company. If a match is uncertain, leave it for me to review."*

  Names and search terms go to that service. Choose which contacts you want researched.

  The Agent may use a separate session for this work. It still needs to check the findings before adding them to your map.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Recover after an incomplete run">
  Ask it to read the saved progress before continuing. Open the latest files to see what they contain.

  A failed request may still have saved useful work. Repeated attempts will not fix missing access or a fixed limit on that run.
</Accordion>
