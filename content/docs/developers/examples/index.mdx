Think about a job you would like help with more than once. These examples start with an everyday request and show how it can grow.

You can adapt the words to your own work. They are examples, not extra templates in the app.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Stay on top of email" icon="mail" href="/developers/examples/email">
    Find messages that need you, prepare replies, and follow up during hiring.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Organize customer documents" icon="folders" href="/developers/examples/document-filing">
    Put emailed proposals and documents where your team can find them.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Find people you know" icon="users" href="/developers/examples/people-graph">
    Turn past conversations into a useful, searchable contact map.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Help with the team wiki" icon="book-open" href="/developers/examples/custom-skills">
    Find the right page, improve it, and teach the Agent your team's approach.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Each job depends on the apps you connect and the access you allow. Try one useful task before you ask for a regular routine.

For a longer example with separate working sessions, see [Review a launch](/developers/examples/release-review).
