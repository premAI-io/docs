title: Stay on top of email
icon: mail
description: Find what needs your attention, then prepare replies you can review.
---

After a busy day, the hard part is often deciding which messages still need you.

An inbox Agent can read the recent conversation, identify an unanswered request, and help you write a reply.

Connect [Google Workspace](/integrations/gmail) and select it in the Agent's [app access](/developers/guides/settings#grant-app-access).

## Give it a clear job

In **New Agent**, describe the help you want:

> *"Help me stay on top of email. Find messages that need a decision or reply from me. Explain why each one matters and suggest a response. Don't change my mailbox."*

Start its working conversation with a small request:

> *"Look at yesterday's mail. What still needs me? Leave out anything that was resolved later in the conversation."*

The result should help you choose what to do next. Check a few source conversations, including one the Agent says needs no action.

## Shape the replies

Ask for a draft in the same conversation:

> *"Draft a reply to this one. Thank them for the proposal and ask whether the price includes setup. Keep it warm and brief."*

Then correct anything that sounds unlike you:

> *"That's too formal. Make it sound like a quick reply to someone I already work with."*

When you like the approach, ask **Manager** to save it:

> *"Use the reply style we settled on in my main conversation. Keep drafts short and natural. Ask me before promising a date I haven't confirmed."*

## Keep drafts where you need them

When you want drafts in Gmail:

> *"Save these replies as drafts in the original Gmail conversations. Keep everyone who needs the reply. Don't send them."*

Saving drafts changes Gmail, so the connection needs **Allowed** access. Review [approval settings](/features/approvals) before you expand what the Agent can do.

After a useful first review, you might ask:

> *"Every weekday at 9 AM, review mail since your last check. Bring me new decisions and unfinished replies. Don't make another draft for something you've already handled."*

Check the time and timezone in **Routines**.

<Accordion title="Use the same approach for hiring">
  Give a Hiring Agent the role, candidate, interview length, and calendars it may check.

  > *"Draft a note asking Maya when she's free for a 30-minute interview next week. Offer Monday or Wednesday afternoon. I'll review it before sending."*

  After you send it:

  > *"Check for her reply every 30 minutes until Friday. When she replies, check both interviewers' calendars and draft a confirmation. Stop checking after that."*

  Review the routine's timing and stopping point. If a calendar is unavailable, the Agent should ask rather than assume a free slot.

  Checking a calendar does not book the interview. Decide who will send the confirmation and create the invitation.
</Accordion>
